Privacy Policy – SpillIt

This privacy policy applies to the SpillIt app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by Maikel Jaspers (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Your Google account display name and email address (if you choose to sign in with Google)
  • A unique user identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication
  • AI-generated question packs you create and choose to save
  • Feedback messages you voluntarily submit through the Application

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional in-app purchases, including premium question packs and AI-generated pack creation. All payment transactions are processed exclusively by Google Play. The Service Provider does not collect, store, or have access to any payment or billing information. For details on how Google handles payment data, please refer to the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Artificial Intelligence Features

The Application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to generate custom question packs based on keywords provided by the user. These keywords and the generated content are processed via Google Firebase Cloud Functions and the Anthropic API. The Service Provider does not use your content to train AI models. All AI processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws. If you have questions about the AI features or data processing, please contact the Service Provider.
